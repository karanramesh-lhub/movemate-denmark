## Verified Information

For non-EU citizens, a valid work or residence permit from SIRI or the Danish Immigration Service is required before applying for CPR registration at ICS East.

Non-EU citizens who plan to stay in Denmark for more than three consecutive months can apply for CPR registration.

The ICS East guidance states that non-EU citizens must apply for CPR registration within six months from the date their work and residence permit was issued and no later than five days after moving into their Danish residence.

For a CPR application at ICS East, relevant documents for non-EU citizens include a passport, proof of address such as a rental contract or housing confirmation, and a valid work or residence permit issued by SIRI or the Danish Immigration Service.

The ICS East guidance states that MitID can be requested in connection with CPR registration.

The exact requirements and applicable process depend on the person's circumstances and should be checked against current official guidance.
