## Verified Information

People coming to Denmark for the first time to work or live need to handle several practical matters.

For people coming to Denmark to work, the required personal identification depends on the intended duration of the stay. A person may need either a CPR number (civil registration number) or a personal tax number.

For people coming to Denmark to work for more than three months, a CPR number is generally required. For EU, EEA and Swiss citizens, the corresponding threshold is generally six months.

People who intend to have income in Denmark need a Danish tax card.

The Danish Tax Agency also identifies NemKonto and MitID as important practical matters for people coming to Denmark.

For a tax card and personal tax number application, relevant documentation can include an employment contract, photo identification and, for citizens of countries outside the EU, Norway, Iceland, Switzerland or Liechtenstein, a Danish residence and work permit.

The exact requirements depend on the person's circumstances and should be checked against the current official guidance.
