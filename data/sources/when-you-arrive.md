## Verified Information

International newcomers may need to handle several practical matters after arriving in Denmark.

People moving to Denmark need to register in the Danish Civil Registration System when applicable.

Finding a home and obtaining a permanent address is an important part of the settlement process. CPR registration generally requires that the person has found a place to live and actually lives there.

International Citizen Service centres provide access to several authorities involved in entry and settlement matters.

For CPR registration, relevant documentation can include a residence and work permit for people who are citizens outside the EU/EEA, the Nordic region or Switzerland, a passport or personal ID, and proof of an address in Denmark such as a rental contract.

A CPR number is used in many practical situations in Denmark, including receiving salary, transferring money, renting a home, opening a Danish bank account, obtaining insurance and accessing healthcare.

The exact requirements depend on the person's circumstances and should be checked against current official guidance.
